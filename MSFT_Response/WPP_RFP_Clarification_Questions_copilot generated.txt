Introduction
This document presents a comprehensive set of clarification questions arising from WPP's Enterprise Agent Capability Framework Vendor Brief (dated 31 March 2026). These questions are organised by brief section and are designed to ensure our response fully addresses WPP's requirements, architecture constraints, and strategic vision.
Each question references the specific section(s) of the RFP brief to enable efficient review by the AI CoE, WPP Enterprise Technology team. We recommend addressing these questions during the Vendor Q&A window (1-15 April 2026) to enable the most complete and aligned response by the 23 April 2026 deadline.
Total questions: The document contains questions across 10 thematic sections covering all aspects of the brief from partnership scope through to commercial terms.

The questions above are intended to eliminate ambiguity and ensure alignment between our proposal and WPP's expectations. We recommend the following approach:
1. Prioritise Control Plane and governance questions (Sections 2, 5) as these have the highest scoring weight and represent the most architecturally significant decisions.
2. Clarify POC scope and logistics (Section 9) early to enable solution architecture design work to begin in parallel with the written response.
3. Confirm technology landscape details (Section 4) - particularly multi-tenant identity, cloud hosting preferences, and existing observability infrastructure - to ensure our architecture diagrams are accurate.
4. Share Addendum A and Addendum B if not yet distributed, as the POC solution architecture deliverables depend heavily on the detailed requirements therein.
5. Schedule a technical deep-dive session to walk through the Control Plane UX requirements and existing dashboard/tooling landscape.
