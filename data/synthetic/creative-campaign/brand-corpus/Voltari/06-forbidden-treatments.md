# Voltari — forbidden treatments

## Visual

- Drivers / passengers visible in or around the vehicle.
- Pure white backgrounds (Tesla-coded).
- Vehicle "doing tricks" (drift, drag, donut). Engineering, not
  spectacle.
- Beach / vacation / lifestyle scenes.
- Side-by-side comparisons with named OEMs.
- Lens flares, vintage filters, anamorphic crops.
- AI-rendered humans anywhere in or near the vehicle.

## Verbal

- "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "disruptive".
- Direct competitor naming ("Tesla-killer", "challenger to BMW").
- "Eco" / "green" in any unqualified form.
- "Ludicrous" (Tesla-coded).
- "Luxury EV" — Voltari is precision EV, not luxury EV.
- Range claims without WLTP qualifier in market-specific wording.
- Carbon claims without "verified by TÜV Süd" attribution.

## Strategic

- Owner-personality framing (Tesla-coded).
- Vehicle-as-lifestyle (Lucid-coded).
- Tech-bro / Silicon Valley pacing.
- "Future of mobility" futurist register — Voltari is a maker now,
  not a futurist.
