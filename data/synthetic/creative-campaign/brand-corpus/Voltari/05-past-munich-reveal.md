# Voltari — Munich Reveal 2025 learnings

## Brief recap

Initial product reveal in Q3 2025. Audience: enthusiast EV
early-adopter 35-54 in UK + DE. KPIs: +22% awareness, +12% test-drive
bookings.

## What worked

- **Munich Werksviertel-Mitte at dawn** as the hero environment.
  Industrial brick + the vehicle on wet asphalt. Awareness lift +27%.
- **No-driver hero film.** 45 seconds of the vehicle moving through
  Munich, never a person on screen. Tested significantly higher in
  recall vs the with-driver alternative cut (which Lucid's brand team
  was openly imitating that quarter).
- **Quantitative supers** ("550 mile WLTP", "0.21 Cd", "carbon-negative
  manufacture, verified by TÜV Süd") inline at the bottom of every
  hero frame. Built credibility without losing aesthetic.

## What didn't

- **Lifestyle B-roll** (the vehicle parked outside a Bavarian
  Berghotel, a couple unloading skis) — pulled before launch on
  internal review (off-brand, Lucid-coded).
- **"Range without compromise"** tagline — failed legal review in UK
  (ASA guidance: "without compromise" is comparative). Replaced with
  "550 miles, verified".
- **Dark gradient** background on social cuts read as Tesla-clone in
  early focus group. Replaced with warm-white on subsequent assets.

## Carry-forward principles

- Vehicle in engineering habitat > vehicle in lifestyle context.
- Quantitative caption on every hero frame.
- No drivers, no passengers, no lifestyle scenes around the vehicle.
- Warm-white > pure-white background in any composition that needs
  one.
