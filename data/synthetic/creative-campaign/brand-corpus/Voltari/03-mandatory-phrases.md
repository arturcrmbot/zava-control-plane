# Voltari — mandatory phrases & legal

## Mandatory in-frame (rotate at least one)

- "550 mile range"
- "carbon-negative manufacture"
- "designed in Munich"

## Range claim — exact wording per market

| Market | Exact wording |
|---|---|
| UK | "Up to 550 miles WLTP" |
| DE | "Bis zu 885 km WLTP" |
| NO | "Inntil 885 km WLTP" |
| Other EU | "Up to 885 km WLTP" |

The "550 mile" headline is permissible only when paired with the
WLTP qualifier somewhere on the asset. UK regulatory practice (ASA
guidance March 2025) requires the qualifier within visual proximity
of the headline number — same screen / same OOH panel.

## Carbon claim — exact wording

- **Permitted:** "carbon-negative manufacture", "carbon-negative
  per vehicle, third-party verified, scope 1-3 (excluding use-phase)".
- **Forbidden:** "carbon neutral", "100% green", "zero emissions"
  (use-phase tailpipe is zero-emission, but full lifecycle is the
  honest claim and the brand's preferred frame).
- **Verification claim:** include "verified by [TÜV Süd]" on any asset
  making the carbon claim. The verifier name in brackets is not
  swappable; legal-cleared.

## Pricing / incentive language

- "Trade-in incentive" — preferred over "discount", "deal", "offer".
- "Battery health guarantee" — exact phrase, don't paraphrase.
- "Up to" — required prefix for any range, charge time, or trade-in
  value claim.

## Brand attribution

- Wordmark: `Voltari` lockup + Munich axis (a horizontal rule beneath).
- Minimum size: 18pt for digital, 12mm for print.
- Wordmark colour: graphite `#0A1628`. Never inverted on warm white.
