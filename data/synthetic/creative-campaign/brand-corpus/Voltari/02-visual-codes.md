# Voltari — visual codes

## Palette

- **Primary:** graphite `#0A1628`, glacier `#C0C8D4`, ember `#4A6DB5`.
- **Secondary:** warm white `#F5F2EE` (warm, not Tesla cold), copper
  accent `#A8643C` (used sparingly, only on technical detail callouts).
- **Forbidden:** pure white backgrounds (Tesla), neon greens (eco
  cliché), high-contrast blacks (luxury OEM cliché).

## Composition

- **Hero stills:** vehicle in three-quarter view, never head-on. Pewter
  or asphalt surface beneath. No driver visible (interior may show
  empty steering wheel, never hands).
- **Motion:** Alpine pass, factory courtyard, Munich industrial district
  at golden hour. Never Highway 1, never beach roads.
- **Detail:** charge port close-up, motor cutaway, paint surface macro.
  Always with one quantitative caption (drag coefficient, charge time).

## Typography

- **Display:** Voltari Grotesk (proprietary; sans-serif, geometric,
  generous letter-spacing).
- **Body:** Inter for technical copy. Tabular figures throughout.
- **Numbers:** never abbreviated. "550 miles", not "550mi".

## Photography rules

- Engineering context preferred over lifestyle. The vehicle in its
  habitat (factory, test track) > the vehicle in someone's life.
- Natural light; no studio strobes for hero. Overcast > golden hour for
  the vehicle's surface (less reflection bias).
- Driver: always cropped or absent. Interior shots: never with a
  person in the seat.

## Forbidden treatments

- Lens flares, anamorphic widescreen, vintage filters.
- Beach / vacation / coastal lifestyle scenes.
- Vehicle "doing tricks" (drift, drag race, donut). Engineering, not
  spectacle.
- Comparison stills against other OEMs (legally + off-brand).
- AI-rendered humans in or around the vehicle.
