# Voltari — brand voice

Voltari is a German EV maker founded in Munich in 2019, defined by
**precision engineering**, **carbon-negative manufacture**, and the
specific claim of **550 mile range from a 2025 production vehicle**.
The brand's voice is understated technical confidence — a maker, not
a marketer.

## Voice principles

- **Numbers over adjectives.** Range, charge time, drag coefficient.
  The vehicle's specs do the talking. No "revolutionary".
- **Engineering register.** German-engineering pacing. Sentences are
  precise; comparisons are quantitative.
- **Munich, not Silicon Valley.** Voltari is not a "tech company". The
  voice is closer to Bauhaus + BMW than to Tesla.
- **Quiet confidence about sustainability.** The carbon-negative claim
  is a fact, not a virtue signal. State it once; don't decorate it.

## Lexicon — preferred

- "550 mile range" (always specific, never "long range")
- "carbon-negative manufacture" (not "carbon neutral", not "green")
- "designed in Munich"
- "precision-machined"
- "single-pedal driving"
- "trade-in incentive" (specific over "great deal")

## Lexicon — forbidden

- "revolutionary", "game-changing", "disruptive"
- "tesla-killer", "challenger" — never compare directly to other OEMs
- "eco" / "green" (legally suspect + off-register)
- "ludicrous" (Tesla-coded)
- "AI-powered" (we don't lead on AI marketing)
- "luxury EV" — we are precision EV, not luxury EV

## Forbidden creative

- **People driving the vehicle.** Vehicle is the protagonist; driver is
  always cropped or absent.
- **Tesla-style minimalism.** Voltari uses warm neutrals, not Tesla
  white-on-white.
- **Lifestyle/aspiration shots** (vehicle parked at a beach house).
  The vehicle is shown in motion, in engineering contexts (test track,
  Munich factory courtyard, Alpine pass), or as a product still.
