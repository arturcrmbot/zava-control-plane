# Voltari — market & jurisdiction notes

## Markets

- **DE** — primary, home market. Bavarian dealer network.
- **UK** — secondary. ASA-tightened EV claims since March 2025.
- **NO** — high EV penetration; competitive against Polestar, Tesla.
  Mandatory Norwegian-language CTV and OOH copy.
- **NL** — emerging. Grant-eligibility messaging permitted.

## Jurisdiction-specific copy rules

| Market | Range claim wording | Carbon claim attribution |
|---|---|---|
| UK | "Up to 550 miles WLTP" | "verified by TÜV Süd" |
| DE | "Bis zu 885 km WLTP" | "geprüft von TÜV Süd" |
| NO | "Inntil 885 km WLTP" | "verifisert av TÜV Süd" |
| NL | "Tot 885 km WLTP" | "geverifieerd door TÜV Süd" |

## Channel constraints

- **CTV DE / NO:** mandatory subtitles. No music with English-language
  vocals.
- **OOH UK:** WLTP qualifier within same panel as headline range
  number (ASA proximity rule).
- **Spotify NL:** carbon-claim audio must include verifier name in
  voiceover, not just visually.
- **TikTok / Reels:** product-still + spec-overlay format only.
  Avoid "owner POV" videos that read Tesla-coded.

## Production constraints

- **No celebrity drivers.** No celebrity passengers. No celebrity
  voiceovers.
- **Music licensing:** original score or instrumental classical only.
  No licensed pop. No AI-composed music.
- **Field production:** Bavarian factory locations whitelisted by
  the atelier. Alpine pass routes pre-cleared. Highway 1 (US),
  beach roads, and Tesla-adjacent locations (Fremont, etc.) all
  blacklisted.
