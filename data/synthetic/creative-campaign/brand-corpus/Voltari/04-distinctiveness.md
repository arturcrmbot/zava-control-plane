# Voltari — distinctiveness benchmark

## Competitive set

- **Tesla** — minimalist white, ludicrous mode, owner-personality brand.
- **Lucid** — California luxury, vehicle-as-lifestyle, glossy.
- **Polestar** — Scandinavian minimalism, fashion-collab adjacency.
- **BMW i / Mercedes EQ** — heritage luxury OEM cues.

## Where Voltari must remain distinct

| Code | Voltari owns | Avoid (read as competitor) |
|---|---|---|
| Palette | Graphite + glacier + ember | Pure white (Tesla), Volvo-blue (Polestar) |
| Surface | Matte paint, asphalt context | High-gloss showroom (Lucid) |
| Typography | Voltari Grotesk + Inter | Optima or Grotesque (Mercedes) |
| Number format | "550 mile range" inline | "Range: 550" datasheet style |
| Composition | 3/4 view, vehicle in motion or product still, no driver | Vehicle parked at lifestyle location (Lucid) |
| Voice | Engineering register, German pacing | Owner-personality (Tesla), fashion-house (Polestar) |

## Distinctiveness rubric

High when:

- ≥3 owned codes present.
- Quantitative caption visible (range, drag, charge time).
- No driver in or around the vehicle.
- Setting is engineering context (factory, test track, alpine pass)
  not lifestyle context.

Low when:

- The composition could swap the wordmark for "Tesla" / "Lucid" /
  "Polestar" without anyone noticing.
- A driver is visible (Tesla-coded) or the vehicle is parked at a beach
  house (Lucid-coded).
- The grade is pure-white-on-white (Tesla) or Volvo-blue-on-pine
  (Polestar).
