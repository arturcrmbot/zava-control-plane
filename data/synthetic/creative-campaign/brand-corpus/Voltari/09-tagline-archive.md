## Tagline rubric

- Numbers preferred over adjectives.
- Engineering register; German pacing.
- No comparatives, no superlatives.
- No reference to other OEMs.
- Periods as rhythm device permitted.
- Two-clause structure permitted ("Designed in Munich. Driven everywhere.").
