# Voltari — messaging pillars

Three pillars. Lead with one per asset.

## 1. Range (550 mile / 885 km WLTP)

- The product's headline number. Most retail-conversion-coded.
- Lead pillar for: hero film, OOH headline, product page hero.
- Pair with: "verified by TÜV Süd" carbon line in same composition.

## 2. Manufacture (carbon-negative, Munich)

- The brand's institutional credibility. Most earned-media-coded.
- Lead pillar for: B2B / fleet, journalist briefings, factory tours.
- Pair with: precision-engineering motifs (motor cutaway, paint
  macro).

## 3. Drive (single-pedal, precision)

- The driving experience. Most enthusiast-coded.
- Lead pillar for: enthusiast press, motoring titles, social cuts.
- Pair with: Alpine pass / test-track footage, no driver visible.

## Pillar pairing

- Range + Manufacture = full brand expression. Tentpole campaigns.
- Range + Drive = enthusiast tilt. Use for press launches and motoring
  titles.
- Manufacture + Drive = quiet sustainability + driving feel.
- All three = overstuffed; flag for cut.

## Not a pillar

- "Tech" — we are not a tech company. Don't lead on the touchscreen.
- "Connectivity" — we don't lead on app features. They exist; they
  don't drive purchase.
- "AI" — we don't market the autonomy stack. It's there; it's not
  the brand.
