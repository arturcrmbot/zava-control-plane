# Verdaire — distinctiveness benchmark

## Competitive set

- **Goop** — wellness lifestyle, millennial pink, celebrity adjacency.
- **Glossier** — minimalist pink, "you but better" positioning.
- **Aesop** — apothecary austere, amber bottles.
- **The Ordinary** — laboratory grade, white-on-grey aesthetic.

## Where Verdaire must remain distinct

| Code | Verdaire owns | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Surface | Linen / soil / lab | Marble / chrome (Goop / Glossier) |
| Palette | Moss + sage + milk | Pink (Goop / Glossier), Pure white (The Ordinary) |
| Setting | Hampshire farm | LA wellness studio (Goop) |
| Voice | Material-led, conversational | Aspirational benefit-led (Glossier) |
| People | Hands-in-soil context | Models with product (Glossier) |
| Tagline format | Origin + period | Mood-led ("a moment for you" — Goop) |

## Distinctiveness rubric

High when:

- ≥3 owned codes present.
- The farm or the lab is visible / implied.
- Material specificity in the copy (named ingredient + region).
- People (when present) are working, gloved, hands-on-soil.

Low when:

- Composition swaps to marble or pink and the wordmark could swap to
  Glossier.
- Copy uses wellness register ("ritual", "glow", "moment for yourself").
- Models pose with product instead of working with material.
