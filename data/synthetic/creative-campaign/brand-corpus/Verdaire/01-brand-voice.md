# Verdaire — brand voice

Verdaire is a regenerative skincare house founded in Hampshire in 2020,
defined by **soil-to-skin traceability**, **regenerative agriculture**,
and **no synthetic preservatives**. The brand is conversational,
material-led, and proof-driven — never wellness-influencer-coded.

## Voice principles

- **Material-led, not benefit-led.** Lead with the ingredient and where
  it came from, not what it does to your skin. ("Calendula, grown on
  the eight acres south of Winchester" beats "Calendula for radiant
  skin".)
- **Conversational, not aspirational.** First-person plural ("we",
  "our") permitted. Second-person ("you", "your skin") permitted in
  DTC product copy but not in hero film.
- **Proof over promise.** Cite the farm, the harvest, the year, the
  shelf-life-without-synthetics. Numbers preferred over adjectives.
- **No wellness register.** Verdaire is not Goop, not Clinique. The
  brand is a *farm with a lab*, not a *spa with a brand*.

## Lexicon — preferred

- "soil-to-skin"
- "regenerative agriculture"
- "no synthetic preservatives"
- "Hampshire farm"
- "single-harvest batch"
- "shelf-life: 90 days, refrigerated"

## Lexicon — forbidden

- "Glow", "radiance", "luminous" (wellness register)
- "Self-care", "ritual", "intention"
- "Clean beauty" (regulatory ambiguity + competitor language)
- "Anti-ageing" (regulatory + brand: we don't promise outcomes)
- "Powered by nature" (cliché)
- "Doctor-formulated" — we have a chief biochemist, not doctors;
  use her name when an authority cite is required.
