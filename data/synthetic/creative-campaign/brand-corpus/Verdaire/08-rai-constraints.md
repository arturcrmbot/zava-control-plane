# Verdaire — RAI constraints

## Hard rules

1. **No AI-rendered human faces or hands.** People in Verdaire creative
   are real — Hampshire farm staff, never AI-generated.
2. **No before/after skin transformations** under any circumstances.
3. **No medical-claim suggestions** — even visually (no white-coat
   doctor figures, no clinical-trial graphs).
4. **No competitor brand styling.** AI-generated visuals must read
   Verdaire-coded (moss/sage/milk + farm or lab context), never
   Glossier/Goop-coded.
5. **No simulated harvest.** Every farm scene must be from the actual
   Hampshire farm, photographed; never generated.

## Soft rules (flag)

- Render that lands in pink-coded palette → flag (Glossier-coded).
- Render with marble/chrome surface → flag (Goop-coded).
- Render with model posing with product → flag (Glossier-coded).
- Caption that reaches for wellness register → flag for copy review.

## Prompt-engineering notes

Prepend prompts with:

> "Documentary still for a regenerative skincare house. Product on
> linen or directly on soil/leaves. Moss/sage/milk palette. Natural
> overcast light. Working farm context."

Append:

> "No people. No skin close-ups. No marble. No pink. No filters.
> Documentary photography, not lifestyle photography."
