# Verdaire — mandatory phrases & legal

## Mandatory in-frame (rotate at least one)

- "soil-to-skin"
- "regenerative agriculture"
- "no synthetic preservatives"

## Forbidden claims (regulatory)

- **"Anti-ageing"** — banned across UK + EU under cosmetic-claims
  guidance. Replace with shelf-life specificity: "90 days, refrigerated".
- **"Clinically proven"** — only on products with the cited study;
  never as a brand-level claim.
- **"100% natural"** — flagged by ASA + AFNOR. Replace with the
  ingredient-level breakdown: "84% farm-grown ingredients, 16% lab
  preservatives (cited on pack)".
- **"Cures", "treats", "heals"** — medical claims; not permitted on
  cosmetics.

## Mandatory legal disclaimers

- **All markets:** ingredient deck on pack must list every component,
  including water (`Aqua`) and the small lab-preservative fraction.
- **UK:** include CTPA reg number on pack and on DTC product pages.
- **EU:** PIF reference visible to regulator on request, not on pack.
