# Verdaire — visual codes

## Palette

- **Primary:** moss `#2C4A2E`, sage `#7A9B6E`, milk `#E8EFD8`.
- **Secondary:** terracotta `#A85F3C` (used only on packaging accents).
- **Forbidden:** Goop millennial-pink, Clinique medical-white, Glossier
  pastel pink. We are a farm; not a spa.

## Composition

- **Hero stills:** product on undyed linen surface or directly on soil
  / leaves. Never on marble, glass, or chrome.
- **Lifestyle:** the farm itself — wide shots of polytunnels, hands in
  soil, Hampshire fields at dawn. People permitted (unlike Solene),
  but always in working context (gloves, aprons, hand-to-soil).
- **Detail:** ingredient close-up on undyed linen. Drops of formula in
  pipettes. Lab-and-field, not glam-and-glow.

## Typography

- **Display:** Söhne Buch (sans-serif, slightly humanist).
- **Body:** Söhne Buch for body and labels. Single typeface family
  throughout — coherence is the point.
- **Numbers:** tabular figures. Shelf-life and harvest-year are
  prominent.

## Photography rules

- Natural light only. Overcast preferred (the Hampshire weather is the
  honest setting).
- People permitted in working context only — hands-in-soil close-ups,
  gloved technician at the field-side lab.
- No models posing with the product. No before/after. No skin close-ups
  unless they are macro of the ingredient (a calendula petal at 100x),
  not of human skin.

## Forbidden treatments

- Marble surfaces, chrome surfaces, glass shelves.
- "Spa scenes" — robes, eye-masks, cucumber slices.
- Before/after skin transformations.
- AI-rendered humans.
- Soft-focus / dreamy filters. The look is documentary, not fantasy.
