# Verdaire — Spring '25 First Harvest

## What worked

- **Hampshire farm hero film** — 60s on the polytunnels at dawn,
  one technician taking calendula heads, no voiceover until 30s.
  +18% awareness vs +12% goal.
- **"90 days, refrigerated" headline** outperformed "no synthetic
  preservatives" by 2.1x in the trial cohort. The specificity is the
  hook; the brand value is the explanation.
- **Hand-in-soil close-up** OOH outperformed the product-on-linen
  composition by 1.4x on awareness in Manchester pilot.

## What didn't

- **Glossier-style model with product** in early carousel iterations
  was pulled — read as wellness/lifestyle, off-brand.
- **"Glow that lasts"** caption failed both legal review (implied
  benefit) and brand review (wellness register). Replaced with
  "Calendula. Single harvest. 90 days."
- **Soft-focus filter on social cuts** read as fantasy, not
  documentary. Replaced with un-filtered.

## Carry-forward

- The farm IS the brand asset. Hero in the field > hero in the studio.
- Specific numbers > qualitative claims.
- Documentary register; no soft focus, no filters.
- People in working context only.
