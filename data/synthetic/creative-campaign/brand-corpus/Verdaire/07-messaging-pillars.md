# Verdaire — messaging pillars

## 1. Provenance (lead pillar)

The Hampshire farm. The named field. The harvest year. The named
biochemist. Material specificity is the brand's whole value.

## 2. Honesty

The 84/16 split (farm-grown vs lab preservative). The 90-day shelf
life. The numbers we publish that competitors don't. Honest is
distinct in a wellness-cliché category.

## 3. Working register

People with their hands in things. Aprons. Gloves. Field labs. The
farm as a workplace, not a Goop-coded retreat.

## Pillar pairing

- Provenance + Honesty = institutional credibility (PR, industry).
- Provenance + Working = brand storytelling (hero film).
- All three = overstuffed.

## Not a pillar

- "Beauty results" — we don't promise outcomes.
- "Wellness" — wrong register.
- "Luxury" — we are precision, not luxury.
- "Innovation" — we are slow craft, not innovation.
