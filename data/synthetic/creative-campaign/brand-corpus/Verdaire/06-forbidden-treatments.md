# Verdaire — forbidden treatments

## Visual

- Marble, chrome, glass-shelf surfaces.
- Spa scenes (robes, eye masks, cucumber slices, candles).
- Before/after skin transformations.
- Models posing with product.
- Soft-focus / "wellness" filters.
- Bath / shower / bathroom-as-sanctuary scenes.

## Verbal

- "Glow", "radiance", "luminous" (wellness)
- "Self-care", "ritual", "intention", "moment for yourself"
- "Clean beauty"
- "Anti-ageing", "anti-wrinkle" (regulatory)
- "Cures", "treats", "heals" (medical claims)
- "Powered by nature" (cliché)

## Strategic

- Wellness-influencer collabs (off-brand register).
- Beauty-editor placement in pure beauty titles (we want
  food/farming/design adjacency: *Cereal*, *Apartamento*, not *Vogue
  Beauty*).
- Celebrity testimonials.
- Subscription box bundling with non-Verdaire products.
