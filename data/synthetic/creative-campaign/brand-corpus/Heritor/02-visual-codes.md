# Heritor — visual codes

## Palette

- **Primary:** ink `#0F0F0E`, brass `#A88554`, parchment `#E6DFD0`.
- **Secondary:** Geneva blue `#1F3F5E` (movement plates only).
- **Forbidden:** any modern luxury "Apple Watch" white-on-aluminium
  palette. No rose-gold-on-blush.

## Composition

- **Hero stills:** macro of the watch face or movement, on parchment
  or ink-coloured surface. Magnification preferred — the audience wants
  to see the finishing.
- **Heritage:** archival photography, hand-tinted prints from the
  Heritor archive. New stills are colour-graded to match.
- **Atelier:** Geneva workshop; the watchmaker's bench, loupe, hands
  at the movement. Hands permitted (unlike Solene/Voltari) — they're
  the watchmaker's hands.

## Typography

- **Display:** Hatton (serif, lapidary) for headlines and product
  names.
- **Body:** Söhne for editorial copy.
- **Numbers:** old-style figures throughout. Reference numbers in
  small caps.

## Photography rules

- Macro is the brand's signature. Get into the movement.
- Hands of the master watchmaker permitted, named in caption.
- No models wearing the watch in a lifestyle scene. The watch lives
  on the bench, in the case, or in macro. Wearer's wrist is a
  product-detail close-up only.
- Archival material (1875-2000) is the brand's most powerful asset.
  Use it.

## Forbidden treatments

- Lifestyle shots with celebrity wearers.
- Watch-on-yacht / watch-at-Monaco-racing scenes (luxury cliché).
- "Smart watch" interface mockups (off-category).
- Comparison shots with other watch brands.
- AI-rendered hands or watchmakers — Heritor's hands are real, named.
