## Tagline rubric

- Heritage cue (year, place) preferred.
- Watchmaker name + calibre permitted.
- Numbers (edition size, hours-per-movement) preferred.
- No comparatives, no superlatives.
- No second-person address.
- Periods as rhythm device permitted; line breaks too.
