# Heritor — mandatory phrases & legal

## Mandatory in-frame (rotate at least one)

- "150 years in Geneva"
- "Hand-finished"
- "Limited edition of [number]" (with the actual number)

## Heritage attribution

- Founding year (1875) appears on every wordmark application.
- "Established Geneva 1875" required on the cap or strap of every
  product still.

## Movement attribution

- Each piece's movement carries a calibre number. Hero copy must
  cite the calibre and the named watchmaker who finished it.
- Permitted: "Calibre H414, finished by Alain Bonnet, 5,400 hours
  per movement".
- Forbidden: "Swiss-made" alone — we are Geneva-made, which is a
  stricter regulatory category. Always say "Geneva-made" or
  "Hallmark of Geneva certified".

## Pricing language

- We don't lead with price in any creative.
- "Investment piece" is forbidden.
- "Heirloom" is permitted in archival / wedding-edition contexts only.

## Forbidden claims (regulatory + brand)

- "Best in class", "world-class", "the finest" — superlative.
- "Lifetime guarantee" — we offer a 5-year service plan, not a
  lifetime guarantee. Cite exact terms.
- "Investment-grade", "appreciating asset" — financial-claim risk.
