# Heritor — forbidden treatments

## Visual

- Lifestyle shots with celebrity wearers.
- Yacht / Monaco / ski lodge / private jet scenes.
- Wrist-on-cuff Manhattan rooftop / boardroom shots.
- Smart-watch interface mockups (off-category).
- AI-rendered hands or watchmakers.
- Comparison shots with other watch brands.
- Side-by-side with non-Heritor pieces.

## Verbal

- "Disruptive", "innovative", "next generation".
- "Smart watch" / "connected".
- "Affordable luxury", "entry luxury", "approachable".
- "Investment piece", "investment-grade", "appreciating asset".
- "Best in class", "world-class", "the finest".
- "Lifetime guarantee" (we offer 5-year service).
- "Swiss-made" alone (always Geneva-made / Hallmark of Geneva).

## Strategic

- Smart-watch comparison campaigns.
- Co-branded editions with non-watch luxury houses.
- Celebrity wearers as creative protagonist.
- Aviation / car / boat editions (off-brand).
- Direct response / "Buy now" CTA in any hero asset.
