# Heritor — messaging pillars

## 1. Heritage

150 years in Geneva. The 1875 founding date. The Reference 414. The
archive.

## 2. Hand-finishing

The 5,400 hours per movement. The named master watchmaker. The
Geneva atelier. The macro of the finishing.

## 3. Scarcity

The numbered edition. The waiting list. The boutique-only
distribution.

## Pillar pairing

- Heritage + Hand-finishing = institutional credibility.
- Heritage + Scarcity = collector-targeted (Hodinkee, WSJ Magazine).
- Hand-finishing + Scarcity = atelier-dispatch / new-edition launches.

## Not a pillar

- "Innovation" — we are 150 years old, not a startup.
- "Connectivity" — we don't make smart watches.
- "Style" — Heritor is a watchmaker, not a fashion brand.
- "Sport" — we don't sponsor F1 or Wimbledon.
