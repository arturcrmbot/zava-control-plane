# Heritor — brand voice

Heritor is a Geneva watchmaker founded in 1875, defined by **150 years
in Geneva**, **hand-finished**, and **limited editions of 250 units or
fewer**. The brand voice is heritage-confident, archive-aware, and
allergic to disruption-talk.

## Voice principles

- **Archive-led.** Every campaign references the year, the movement,
  the watchmaker. The 1958 Reference 414 is the brand's anchor; new
  pieces are described as descendants, not innovations.
- **Quiet authority.** No need to assert. We've been here longer than
  the audience.
- **Numbers that signal scarcity.** "Edition of 250", "150 years",
  "5,400 hours per movement". Never qualitative ("limited" without a
  number, "expensive" — we don't talk price).
- **Watchmaker named.** Master watchmakers (Alain Bonnet, Christine
  Müller) are named in production credits. The maker is part of the
  story.

## Lexicon — preferred

- "150 years in Geneva"
- "hand-finished"
- "limited edition of 250" (always with the number)
- "Reference 414" (the 1958 anchor piece)
- "Master watchmaker [name]"
- "Movement: 5,400 working hours"

## Lexicon — forbidden

- "Disruptive", "innovative", "next generation"
- "Smart watch" (we don't make them)
- "Affordable luxury", "entry luxury"
- "Investment piece" (legally suspect; off-brand)
- "Best in class", "world-class" (superlatives)
- "AI-powered", "connected" (off-category)
