# Heritor — market & jurisdiction notes

## Markets

- **CH** — primary, home market. Atelier-direct + boutique.
- **UK / FR** — secondary. Mayfair + Place Vendôme boutiques.
- **US** — growth. WSJ Magazine + collector circuit.
- **JP** — high-collector market. Ginza boutique.

## Channel constraints

- **Print:** *Wall Street Journal Magazine*, *Hodinkee Magazine*,
  *Revolution*, *T: The New York Times Style Magazine*. Whitelist
  curated by atelier comms.
- **Digital:** *Hodinkee.com* (article placement preferred over banner).
  No programmatic display anywhere — degrades brand context.
- **Boutique:** all hero creative shipped as 30x42 cm prints to
  CH/UK/FR/US/JP boutiques 14 days before campaign launch.
- **Watch fairs:** Watches & Wonders Geneva is the tentpole. No
  Baselworld presence (institutional decision 2019).

## Production constraints

- **All creative produced in Geneva or in Heritor-owned space.** No
  on-location lifestyle shoots.
- **Master watchmakers** (Alain Bonnet, Christine Müller, Yves
  Lachenal) are named in every production credit.
- **Music licensing:** original chamber-orchestra score only. No
  licensed pop. No AI-composed music.
- **No celebrity ambassadors.** Institutional policy.
