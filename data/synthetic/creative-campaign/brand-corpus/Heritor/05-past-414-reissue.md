# Heritor — Reference 414 Reissue 2024

## Brief recap

The 1958 Reference 414 — the brand's anchor piece — reissued as a
limited edition of 100. Audience: collector 45-65, global. KPIs:
+8% awareness, +15% qualified leads.

## What worked

- **Archive cross-fade hero film** — historic footage of the original
  1958 watchmakers cross-fading to the present-day Geneva atelier,
  with Christine Müller (master watchmaker) named on screen at 0:14.
  +11% awareness vs +8% goal.
- **"Edition of 100. Numbered 1 of 100."** OOH — the specificity drove
  qualified-lead inquiries +21% vs the un-numbered alternative.
- **Print-only earned media** in *The Wall Street Journal Magazine*,
  *Hodinkee*, *Revolution*. No digital echo for the first 14 days.

## What didn't

- **Wristshot lifestyle b-roll** (Manhattan rooftop, watch on cuff)
  was pulled — read as Vacheron-coded. Replaced with macro of the
  movement.
- **"Investment piece"** copy in early DTC drafts failed legal
  review (financial-claim risk). Replaced with "Service plan: 5
  years inclusive".
- **Smart-watch comparison advertisement** (suggested by a digital
  agency partner) was rejected outright — off-category and off-brand.

## Carry-forward

- Archive is the brand's strongest asset. Use it.
- Numbers are the brand's strongest asset after archive. Cite them.
- Watchmakers named on screen / on page increases credibility +
  conversion.
- Macro > lifestyle. Wrist-on-cuff = Vacheron-coded.
