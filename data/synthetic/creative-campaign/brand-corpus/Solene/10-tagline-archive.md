## Tagline rubric (for new candidates)

- ≤3 words preferred (≤2 ideal).
- No superlatives.
- Origin-led (place name, harvest verb, material noun).
- No second-person address ("you", "your").
- No imperatives that ask the customer to feel something.
- Punctuation OK (periods, middle dot) — adds rhythm without copy.
