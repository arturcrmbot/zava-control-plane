# Solene — forbidden treatments

This document consolidates every "do not" from the brand's voice, visual,
and legal docs. The `brand-guardian` skill should treat the presence of
any of these in a concept as a violation worth flagging.

## Visual

- **Human faces in hero stills.** Hands at wrist crop only. No eyes.
- **Glossy / reflective product shots.** Surfaces are matte; reflections
  read as cheap luxury (Tom Ford / Margiela mass).
- **Primary brights, neon, high-saturation pinks/purples.** Off-palette.
- **Cinemagraphs / animated stills.** Read as e-commerce, not editorial.
- **Lens flares, vintage filters, Insta-style colour grading.**
- **Crowds / parties / nightlife / clubs.** Wrong tonal register.

## Verbal

- "100% sustainable" (legal — EU 2024/825)
- "indulge", "treat yourself", "transformative", "best-in-class"
- "Luxury for all" (off-brand register)
- Long-form product names (>3 words; reads as Le Labo)
- Bold typography in body or labels
- Religious symbolism (especially holiday creative)

## Strategic

- **Lifestyle-with-model** compositions (faces / bodies as protagonist).
- **Celebrity endorsements.** Heritage rules.
- **Crowd shots / festival imagery.** Wrong audience.
- **Untested provenance regions.** New origins (Comoros, Calabria) earn
  their own campaign before appearing in hero assets — never tacked on.

## Process

- **AI-rendered humans.** Regulatory + brand: never. (When the
  `concept-curator` skill renders a still that contains a person, the
  validator must reject; the orchestrator emits a content_safety
  exception.)
