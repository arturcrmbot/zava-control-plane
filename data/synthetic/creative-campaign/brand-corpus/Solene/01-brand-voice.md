# Solene — brand voice

Solene is a sustainable luxury fragrance house founded in Grasse in 2012,
defined by **single-source botanicals**, **regenerative provenance**, and
**low-impact craftsmanship**. Every line carries the maker's mark of the
soil it came from.

## Voice principles

- **Quiet confidence.** Never shout. The product sells itself; the brand
  says less than the moment requires. Sentences end early.
- **Material-led.** Lead with the botanical, the soil, the maker — not
  the wearer. The fragrance is the protagonist; the wearer is the
  setting.
- **No superlatives.** Avoid "best", "most", "ultimate". Avoid "100%
  sustainable" — runs foul of EU green-claims directive (2024/825).
  Prefer "single-source" or "soil-to-stopper" specificity.
- **Origin over outcome.** Talk about where it comes from, not what it
  does to the wearer. "Cultivated in Provence" beats "transports you to
  Provence".

## Tonal range

| Setting | Tone |
|---|---|
| Hero film | Cinematic, slow, sparse VO. One sentence per beat. |
| OOH | Two words, max. Botanical name + city. |
| Social | Material close-up, no face. Caption: object name + region. |
| DTC site | Composed editorial. Past tense for provenance. Present tense for craft. |

## Lexicon — preferred

- "single-source"
- "soil-to-stopper"
- "regenerative provenance"
- "low-impact craftsmanship"
- "Grasse atelier"
- "first cold extraction"
- "small batch"

## Lexicon — forbidden

- "100% sustainable" (legal)
- "luxury for all" (off-brand)
- "transformative" (overused; off-brand)
- "indulge" (off-brand register)
- "best-in-class" (no superlatives)
