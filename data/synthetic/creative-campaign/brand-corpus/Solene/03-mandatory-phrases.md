# Solene — mandatory phrases & legal

Every campaign asset must include or accommodate the following.

## Mandatory in-frame phrases (rotate at least one)

- "regenerative provenance"
- "soil-to-stopper"
- "single-source botanicals"

## Mandatory legal disclaimers

- All EU markets: **No "100% sustainable" claims.** Replace with
  "regenerative provenance, third-party verified" if a sustainability
  claim is required. Cites EU directive 2024/825.
- France-specific: include `Origine France garantie — département 06` on
  any pack shot used in print or OOH.
- DTC / e-commerce: "Vegan • Cruelty-free" badge in product detail
  pages only — never in hero campaign assets (visual clutter).

## Brand attribution

- Type lockup: `Solene` wordmark + `Grasse, depuis 2012` in 4pt.
- Minimum clear space: equal to the cap-height of `S` in wordmark.
- Wordmark colour: smoke `#3A3530` on light backgrounds. Never inverted
  except on black-and-white festive editions (limited release only).

## Mandatory provenance metadata (digital only)

For any DTC product page or programmatic ad:

- `harvest_year`
- `harvest_region` (down to the commune level)
- `extraction_method` (one of: cold-press, steam-distil, enfleurage)
- `co2e_per_unit_g` (declared)

These appear in the product schema; not the hero creative.
