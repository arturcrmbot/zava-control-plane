# Solene — visual codes

## Palette

- **Primary:** ivory `#F4ECE0`, pewter `#928B7A`, smoke `#3A3530`.
- **Secondary:** vellum `#E0D3BE`, rust (sparingly) `#7A4B2A`.
- **Forbidden:** primary brights, neon, high-saturation pinks/purples.
  Black is reserved for typography only — never as a fill.

## Composition

- **Hero stills:** centred product, one third of frame. Generous negative
  space above. Backdrop matte, never glossy.
- **Lifestyle:** product in environment (atelier table, botanical
  garden, soil close-up). Never staged on a person.
- **Botanical close-up:** rim-lit, single specimen, low contrast. Macro
  detail of leaf veins / petal texture preferred over whole bloom.

## Typography

- **Display:** Apoc Revelations (light) for product names + headlines.
- **Body:** GT Sectra Fine for editorial copy.
- **Labels / caps:** all-lowercase, letter-spaced 0.08em. No bold.

## Photography rules

- Natural light, golden-hour or overcast. No studio strobes for hero.
- Shallow depth of field; product sharp, environment soft.
- Skin tones are absent (no faces in hero stills — see brand voice).
- Allowed motifs: hands tending botanicals (cropped at wrist), shadows
  on pewter surfaces, dawn light on bottles.

## Forbidden treatments

- Cinemagraphs / animated stills (off-register).
- Lens flares, vintage filters, Insta-style colour grading.
- Crowds, parties, clubs, nightlife scenes.
- Celebrity endorsements (Heritage rules — see legal).
- AI-rendered humans (regulatory + brand: never).
