# Solene AW24 Origin — what we learned

## Brief recap

- Autumn/winter 2024, holiday gifting.
- Mandatory message: "regenerative provenance".
- KPIs: gift attach +20%, recall +15%.

## What worked

- **Engravable bottle** mechanic (DTC + Selfridges). Conversion +27%
  vs unengraved hero. The personalisation lifted the gift narrative
  without compromising the brand's restraint.
- **Single-soil edition.** A 1,500-unit run from one specific Comoros
  ylang-ylang field, with the harvester named on the box. Sold out in
  9 days; +3.1x earned media vs the standard hero.
- **Print-only quiet ad** in *The Gentlewoman* and *Apartamento*. No
  digital echo. Read as a statement.

## What didn't

- **Holiday-coded imagery** (snowflakes, gift wrap, ribbons) flagged as
  generic. Replaced with the bottle on a pewter tray, single dried
  immortelle stem, no festive props.
- **Religious symbolism** appeared in two carousel iterations (subtle
  manger silhouette in OOH); pulled within 24h of launch on internal
  review. Lesson: holiday brief constraints must say "no specific
  religious symbolism" explicitly.
- **Regenerative claim wording** in the FR market: original ran
  "100% renouvelable" — flagged by AFNOR as breach of green-claims
  guidance. Reworded to "Origine régénérative · vérifiée tiers".

## Lessons for the next holiday brief

- Gift mechanic > gift imagery. Engravable / single-edition / numbered.
- One stem, one surface, no festive props.
- Pre-clear sustainability copy with legal in every market.
- No religious symbolism — including subtle background shapes.
