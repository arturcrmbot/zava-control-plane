# Solene — audience archetypes

## Core: "The Cultivator"

- **Demographic:** 32-44, urban European (Paris, London, Berlin, Milan).
  Income €120k+, no household children at home, owns 1-2 fragrances at
  any time and replaces them annually.
- **Mindset:** Knows the difference between Aesop and Le Labo. Reads
  *Apartamento*. Values restraint over abundance. Suspicious of
  performance-marketed luxury.
- **Buying triggers:** Origin story they can verify. Limited editions
  with names/numbers. Earned media in *The Gentlewoman* / *Cabana*.
- **Channels:** DTC site (preferred), Selfridges Beauty Hall, Liberty,
  Bon Marché. Social discovery via Pinterest > Instagram > TikTok.
- **Brand-fit signals:** "regenerative", "single-source", "atelier",
  "Grasse", botanical specificity. Allergic to "wellness", "vibes",
  "everyday luxury".

## Adjacent: "The Returning Editor"

- **Demographic:** 45-58, ex-Cultivator who has moved into discovery
  mode again post-children / post-divorce / post-career-pivot.
- **Mindset:** Wants reassurance the brand is the same as when they
  last bought, plus one new edition that lets them re-mark themselves.
- **Buying triggers:** Anniversary editions, archival re-releases,
  named-harvester limited runs.

## Adjacent: "The Inheritor"

- **Demographic:** 28-35, has discovered Solene through a parent or
  partner. Lower income (€60-90k) but high cultural capital.
- **Buying triggers:** Smaller bottles, the engravable mechanic, the
  DTC discovery set (3 × 5ml, +1 free with newsletter signup).
- **Why we serve them anyway:** They become Cultivators in 5-7 years.

## Excluded audiences (not for FY26)

- Gen-Z TikTok-discovery — wrong register, fast burn-rate, low LTV.
- Mass-luxury (Sephora floor traffic) — wrong adjacency.
- Wellness-ritual buyers (Goop / Aesop overlap who shop on category not
  brand) — they don't repeat-buy at our price point.
