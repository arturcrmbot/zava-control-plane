# Solene — market & jurisdiction notes

## Markets

- **France** — primary. Creative review by Paris atelier. All copy
  legalled in FR by AFNOR-certified counsel.
- **UK** — secondary, growth focus. Selfridges + Liberty exclusive
  beats. ASA tightened "sustainable" language Q3 2025.
- **Germany** — emerging. Mandatory `Ohne Tierversuche` label.
- **US** — out of scope for FY26. Do not produce US-coded creative.

## Jurisdiction-specific copy rules

| Jurisdiction | Sustainability claim |
|---|---|
| FR | "Origine régénérative · vérifiée tiers" — never "100% renouvelable" |
| UK | "Regenerative provenance, third-party verified" |
| DE | "Regenerativer Anbau · drittverifiziert"; pair with `Ohne Tierversuche` |

## Channel constraints by market

- **OOH FR:** photography only (no illustration). Two-word maximum copy.
- **OOH UK:** illustration permitted on Tube cross-track (low light).
- **CTV DE:** mandatory subtitle track + `Ohne Tierversuche` end-card.
- **TikTok / Reels / Shorts:** approved for UK + FR only. Forbidden in DE
  pending legal review of in-platform claims.

## Talent / production constraints

- **No celebrities, period.**
- **All production crew above-line:** EU-resident, French-speaking
  preferred. Atelier must approve director.
- **Music licensing:** classical / contemporary classical only. No
  licensed pop. No AI-composed music.
- **Field production:** harvest-aligned only. No simulated harvest
  scenes (regulatory + ethical).
