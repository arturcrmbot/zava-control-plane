# Solene SS24 Renaissance — what we learned

## Brief recap

- Spring/summer 2024, all EU markets.
- Audience: aspirational European 25-44, primary FR + UK.
- KPIs: +12% awareness, +6% intent.

## What worked

- **Hero film** with single-cellist score and dawn light on the Provence
  fields. Slow pan, no VO until 18s. Awareness lift +14% (vs +12% goal).
- **OOH** two-word format (`Solene · Grasse`) outperformed long-form
  copy (`The story of a single rose, captured`) on recall by 2.3x in
  Manchester pilot.
- **No-face still** with hands tending botanicals (cropped at wrist) +52%
  saves vs the lifestyle-with-model version on Instagram.

## What didn't

- **Lifestyle scenes with full-figure models** flagged off-brand by
  internal review and external focus group ("looks like Tom Ford").
  Pulled mid-flight in week 3.
- **"Indulge in Provence" tagline** failed legal review and creative
  review (off-brand register; superlative). Replaced with "Cultivated in
  Provence" mid-campaign.
- **Cinemagraph hero** (animated bottle on rotating plinth) was pulled —
  read as e-commerce asset, not editorial. Brand integrity penalty.

## Carry-forward principles for AW26

- **Botanical protagonist, never the wearer.** Reaffirmed.
- **Silence over sound.** Add 3-5s of held silence before any score
  enters. Don't fill the frame.
- **Hands at wrist crop only.** Faces remain forbidden in hero assets.
- **Provence + Grasse = on-brand.** Other origins (Comoros, Calabria)
  must be earned with a campaign of their own — don't tack on.
