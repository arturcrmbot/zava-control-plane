# Solene — messaging pillars FY26

Three pillars, in order of priority. Every campaign must lead with at
least one; never all three in one asset.

## 1. Provenance (lead pillar)

- Soil, region, harvest, harvester. Story of where it came from.
- Verb tense: past (provenance) → present (craft).
- Sample line: "Cultivated by hand in the Plateau de Valensole, August
  harvest, single-cold extraction within 48 hours."
- Lead pillar for: hero film, OOH, print editorial.

## 2. Craft

- Atelier, maker, technique, time. Story of how it was made.
- Verb tense: present.
- Sample line: "Pressed at our Grasse atelier within two days of harvest."
- Lead pillar for: DTC product page, social close-ups, behind-the-scenes.

## 3. Restraint

- Quietness. Negative space. Single-stem editorial. The opposite of
  modern luxury overstatement.
- Sample line: (often left implicit — restraint is a visual move, not
  a copy beat).
- Lead pillar for: gift editions, festive stillness, anniversary moments.

## Pillar pairing

- Provenance + Craft = full brand expression. Use for tentpole campaigns.
- Provenance + Restraint = reflective / editorial moment.
- Craft + Restraint = atelier dispatch (small-edition launches).
- All three = overstuffed; flag for cut.

## What's NOT a pillar

- Wellness / self-care framing — competitor territory.
- Indulgence / hedonism — off-brand register.
- Status / luxury display — opposite of restraint.
- Innovation / disruption — off-brand. Solene is a maker, not a
  disrupter.
