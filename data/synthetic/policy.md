# WPP Group T&E Policy (Synthetic — POC1)

> Effective 2026-04-01. Applies to all WPP agencies and markets unless market-specific overrides apply. This is the single authoritative source for R/A/G classification of expense claims.

## 1. Scope

Covers meals, travel, accommodation, entertainment, and miscellaneous business expenses incurred by WPP employees in the course of client work or internal operations across the UK, US, DE, and IN markets. Out of scope: relocation expenses, capital equipment purchases, agency-billable production costs, and recharged client expenses (handled via separate client-billing policy).

All monetary thresholds are quoted in the local market currency. Cross-currency claims are converted at the WPP corporate FX rate published on the first business day of the claim month.

## 2. Markets and currencies

| Market | Currency | Tax treatment | Receipt threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK | GBP | VAT receipts required >=GBP 25 | GBP 25 |
| US | USD | Receipts required >=USD 25 | USD 25 |
| DE | EUR | VAT receipts required for all amounts | EUR 0 |
| IN | INR | GST receipts required >=INR 500 | INR 500 |

The "receipt threshold" is the amount at or above which an itemised receipt is mandatory. Below threshold, a self-attested line item is acceptable (see Section 5, auto-reclaim).

## 3. Categories and per-market R/A/G rules

The 110% boundary is hard-coded throughout this section. A claim within 110% of the cap is Amber and requires reviewer attention; a claim above 110% of the cap is Red and is rejected unless an exception is granted.

### 3.1 Meals

| Market | Solo cap | Per-attendee cap | Alcohol | After-hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UK | GBP 40 | GBP 75 | Permitted at client dinner only | Permitted |
| US | USD 50 | USD 75 | Permitted at client dinner only | Permitted |
| DE | EUR 45 | EUR 70 | Prohibited on entertainment | Permitted |
| IN | INR 2,500 | INR 4,000 | Prohibited | Permitted |

110% boundary values (computed once for clarity):

| Market | Solo 110% | Per-attendee 110% |
|---|---|---|
| UK | GBP 44.00 | GBP 82.50 |
| US | USD 55.00 | USD 82.50 |
| DE | EUR 49.50 | EUR 77.00 |
| IN | INR 2,750 | INR 4,400 |

**Green (auto-approve):** within solo cap, with receipt where receipt-threshold applies, attendees <=1, no alcohol; or within per-attendee cap with receipt and named attendees >=2.

**Amber (review):** above the cap but at or below 110% of the cap with receipt; or attendees > per-attendee count claimed; or alcohol present at non-client meal in UK/US; or weekend or public-holiday meal without business reason annotated; or per-attendee claim with only one named attendee.

**Red (breach):** above 110% of cap; alcohol where prohibited (DE entertainment, IN any meal); missing receipt at or above the market receipt threshold; group meal with no attendee names; per-attendee claim with zero named attendees.

### 3.2 Travel

Per-market caps. "Cap" applies to a single trip leg unless otherwise noted.

| Market | Air (economy domestic) | Air (economy long-haul) | Rail (standard) | Taxi single trip | Personal vehicle (per km/mile) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UK | GBP 250 | GBP 900 | GBP 180 | GBP 60 | GBP 0.45 / mile |
| US | USD 400 | USD 1,200 | USD 220 (Acela) | USD 75 | USD 0.67 / mile |
| DE | EUR 300 | EUR 950 | EUR 160 (ICE) | EUR 55 | EUR 0.30 / km |
| IN | INR 12,000 | INR 60,000 | INR 4,500 (AC1) | INR 2,000 | INR 12 / km |

Business-class air is permitted for long-haul flights >=8 hours; the cap is 2.0x the long-haul economy cap (i.e. GBP 1,800 / USD 2,400 / EUR 1,900 / INR 120,000). Business class on flights <8 hours is always Red unless a medical exception is on file.

110% boundary values (long-haul economy):

| Market | 110% long-haul economy |
|---|---|
| UK | GBP 990.00 |
| US | USD 1,320.00 |
| DE | EUR 1,045.00 |
| IN | INR 66,000 |

**Green (auto-approve):** booked in economy at or below cap, with itinerary/booking reference; rail at or below cap; taxi at or below cap and origin/destination both business locations; personal-vehicle mileage at or below the per-km/mile rate, with mileage log.

**Amber (review):** booking above cap but at or below 110% of cap; taxi between business and home address; personal vehicle without mileage log under GBP/USD/EUR 50 / INR 2,000 equivalent; rail-class upgrade explicitly justified for night travel; air booking made within 7 days of travel (premium fare suspect).

**Red (breach):** any leg above 110% of cap; business-class on a flight <8 hours without medical exception; first-class rail (any market) without director-level approval annotated; taxi without origin/destination; personal-vehicle claim with no mileage log at or above the auto-reclaim threshold; ride-hailing tip exceeding 25% of fare.

### 3.3 Accommodation

Per-night caps. Caps are inclusive of room rate and applicable city/tourist taxes; exclusive of restaurant charges (which fall under §3.1 meals).

| Market | Standard city | London / NYC / Mumbai (Tier 1 surcharge) | Other Tier 2 cities |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK | GBP 180 | GBP 280 (London only) | GBP 200 (Manchester, Edinburgh) |
| US | USD 220 | USD 380 (NYC, SF) | USD 260 (LA, Chicago, Boston) |
| DE | EUR 170 | EUR 240 (Munich, Frankfurt) | EUR 190 (Berlin, Hamburg) |
| IN | INR 9,000 | INR 16,000 (Mumbai, Delhi) | INR 11,000 (Bangalore, Hyderabad) |

110% boundary values (Tier 1):

| Market | Tier 1 110% |
|---|---|
| UK | GBP 308.00 |
| US | USD 418.00 |
| DE | EUR 264.00 |
| IN | INR 17,600 |

Weekend stays (Friday or Saturday night) are claimable only if (a) Friday or Monday is a working travel day with a confirmed business meeting, or (b) the Saturday-night stay yields demonstrable airfare savings of >=GBP 100 / USD 150 / EUR 120 / INR 8,000 versus a same-day return. Otherwise the weekend nights are Red.

**Green (auto-approve):** at or below the appropriate market/tier cap, with itemised hotel folio, dates align with confirmed travel itinerary, no in-room movies/spa/laundry charges bundled into the line.

**Amber (review):** above cap but at or below 110% of cap; weekend stay with the airfare-saving justification annotated; tier-1 surcharge claimed for a non-tier-1 city without override note; bundled mini-bar charges under GBP/USD/EUR 20 / INR 800 equivalent.

**Red (breach):** above 110% of cap; weekend stay with no business or airfare-saving justification; in-room entertainment, spa, or laundry charges bundled into the room line without separation; stay extending more than one night beyond the last business meeting without justification.

### 3.4 Entertainment

Entertainment is defined as a hosted event (meal, drinks, sports, theatre, gallery) at which a non-WPP guest (client, prospect, or external partner) is present. Internal-only "entertainment" is reclassified as a meal (§3.1) and judged against meal caps.

| Market | Per-head cap | Alcohol | Maximum WPP-to-guest ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK | GBP 110 | Permitted | 2 WPP per 1 guest |
| US | USD 130 | Permitted | 2 WPP per 1 guest |
| DE | EUR 100 | Prohibited | 2 WPP per 1 guest |
| IN | INR 5,500 | Prohibited | 2 WPP per 1 guest |

110% boundary values (per-head):

| Market | Per-head 110% |
|---|---|
| UK | GBP 121.00 |
| US | USD 143.00 |
| DE | EUR 110.00 |
| IN | INR 6,050 |

Entertainment requires:
- Itemised receipt at any amount (§2 receipt thresholds do not apply — entertainment is always above threshold).
- All attendees named, with their organisation, in the claim narrative.
- A business-purpose line of >=20 characters explaining the commercial rationale.
- No spouse/partner attendance unless the spouse/partner is a representative of the guest organisation.

**Green (auto-approve):** within per-head cap, alcohol rules respected, all attendees named with org, WPP:guest ratio <=2:1, business purpose annotated, itemised receipt attached.

**Amber (review):** above per-head cap but at or below 110% of cap; WPP:guest ratio between 2:1 and 3:1 with seniority justification; business-purpose line 10-19 characters (boundary-vague); event on a weekend or public holiday.

**Red (breach):** above 110% of per-head cap; alcohol where prohibited (DE/IN); WPP:guest ratio >3:1; any guest unnamed or org missing; business-purpose line absent or under 10 characters; spouse/partner attendance without qualifying role; venue is a non-business establishment (e.g. nightclub).

### 3.5 Miscellaneous

Catch-all for stationery, software subscriptions, conference and training fees, working-from-home incidentals, and small one-off purchases. Anything not classifiable under §3.1-3.4 falls here.

| Sub-category | UK | US | DE | IN |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stationery / single purchase | GBP 50 | USD 60 | EUR 50 | INR 2,500 |
| Software subscription / month | GBP 30 | USD 40 | EUR 30 | INR 2,000 |
| Conference / training fee | GBP 1,500 | USD 2,000 | EUR 1,600 | INR 80,000 |
| WFH incidental / month | GBP 25 | USD 30 | EUR 25 | INR 1,500 |

110% boundary values (conference/training, the most-claimed sub-category):

| Market | Conference 110% |
|---|---|
| UK | GBP 1,650.00 |
| US | USD 2,200.00 |
| DE | EUR 1,760.00 |
| IN | INR 88,000 |

Software subscriptions must be on the WPP-approved vendor list (maintained by IT Procurement). Subscriptions to vendors not on the list are Red regardless of amount, pending IT review and onboarding.

**Green (auto-approve):** within sub-category cap, with receipt where receipt-threshold applies, vendor name recognisable, business purpose annotated for amounts above 50% of cap.

**Amber (review):** above cap but at or below 110% of cap; software vendor not on approved list but recognisable industry tool (e.g. unannounced new vendor); conference fee with no agenda or speaker list attached; WFH incidental claimed for a month in which the employee logged <10 working-from-home days.

**Red (breach):** above 110% of cap; software subscription to an unapproved or unknown vendor; conference fee with no event name/date; stationery claim with no itemisation above receipt threshold; WFH incidental claimed for a month in which the employee was on leave for more than half the working days.

## 4. Receipt and documentation requirements

- Below market receipt threshold: a self-attested line is sufficient (Green eligible).
- At or above threshold: itemised receipt required. Missing receipt = Red unless auto-reclaim applies (§5).
- Receipt must show: vendor name, date, amount, currency, line items if itemised meal/entertainment.
- Foreign-currency receipts must be accompanied by an FX-conversion line referencing the WPP corporate FX rate for the claim month.
- Digital receipts (email PDFs, app screenshots) are accepted provided the vendor name, date, amount, and currency are unambiguously legible. Hand-scribbled paper receipts are accepted up to GBP 100 / USD 130 / EUR 110 / INR 8,000; above these amounts an itemised printed or digital receipt is required.

## 5. Auto-reclaim (cash, no receipt)

Cash transactions below the market receipt threshold qualify for auto-reclaim with a self-attested line containing: amount, date, vendor, business reason. Above threshold and missing receipt = Red.

Auto-reclaim is capped per employee per calendar month:

| Market | Monthly auto-reclaim cap |
|---|---|
| UK | GBP 100 |
| US | USD 120 |
| DE | EUR 100 |
| IN | INR 4,000 |

Once the monthly cap is reached, all further no-receipt cash claims that month are Red regardless of individual line amount.

## 6. Repeat-offender progressive enforcement

Breach count is computed over a rolling 90-day window per employee, across all categories.

| Prior breaches in 90 days | Tier on next breach | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | warning | Reviewer note; employee notified by email; line manager CC'd. |
| 1 | warning | Reviewer note; employee notified; line manager CC'd; HR Business Partner CC'd. |
| 2 | escalation | Reviewer rejection; line-manager 1:1 within 5 working days; HR BP review of last 6 months of claims. |
| 3 or more | major-violation | Reviewer rejection; HR formal review; possible disciplinary action under the WPP Code of Conduct. |

Category-specific overrides (a single breach immediately escalates):
- **Entertainment with prohibited alcohol (DE, IN):** treated as escalation on first occurrence.
- **Business-class on <8h flight without medical exception:** treated as major-violation on first occurrence.
- **Personal-vehicle claim with fabricated mileage log (verified):** treated as major-violation on first occurrence and triggers fraud-team referral.

Tiers reset to "warning" for an employee with no breaches in the prior 180 days.

## 8. Glossary

- **R/A/G:** Red / Amber / Green classification produced by the policy classifier; Red = reject, Amber = reviewer required, Green = auto-approve.
- **Receipt threshold:** market amount at or above which an itemised receipt is mandatory.
- **Auto-reclaim:** mechanism to claim small cash expenses without a receipt, subject to monthly cap (§5).
- **Tier 1 city:** the named city receiving the accommodation Tier 1 surcharge in §3.3.
- **WPP:guest ratio:** count of WPP attendees divided by count of non-WPP attendees at an entertainment event.
- **Rolling 90-day window:** the 90 calendar days preceding the date of the claim under review.
