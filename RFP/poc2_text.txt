1. Purpose
This addendum details the requirements for POC 2: HR Talent Lifecycle Agent Team. It is the frontier POC designed to stress-test the full spectrum of your capability.
The scenario is deliberately complex: multiple business processes, multiple human participants, multiple agent types, multiple surfaces, and multiple jurisdictions.
This is not a HR chatbot. We are asking you to demonstrate an agentic operating model for HR, where agents perform the bulk of operational work and humans supervise, intervene, and decide at critical moments via a Control Plane, not a chat interface.
Honesty about gaps and collaborative solution architecture is valued over marketing claims.
2. The Control Plane: Primary Deliverable
This section takes precedence. A vendor who demonstrates 22 of 23 capabilities brilliantly but fails to propose a Control Plane solution will struggle to pass this POC.
The HR Business Partner in this scenario does not process hiring requests. They govern the agent fleet that processes hiring requests.
Target operating model: 1 HR BP operating a Control Plane across 15ÔÇô20 concurrent hiring workflows spanning multiple agencies, markets, and jurisdictions.
The Control Plane must provide:
Fleet dashboard: All active hiring workflows; status, health, SLA, exception state; filterable by agency, market, risk level, jurisdiction
Exception-only surfacing: Of {15} active workflows, the HR BP sees only the {2ÔÇô3} requiring intervention. The others run autonomously and are invisible unless the HR BP drills in
Instant situational awareness: Click into any workflow and within 5 seconds understand: what the agents have done, what stopped progress, what the agent recommends, what the options are
Bulk HITL: Review and approve batches of similar decisions (e.g., 8 interview schedules, all low-risk) in a single action
Skill amplification: When the HR BP is uncertain (e.g., German works council requirements), the platform proactively surfaces the relevant policy, precedents from similar hires, and a recommended approach
Autonomy dials: Per-workflow and per-decision-type thresholds, adjustable at runtime
Anti-requirement: A Copilot Studio bot, a Teams approval card, or an email notification chain is not a Control Plane. MS Teams and email are surfaces: they deliver alerts from the Control Plane; they are not a substitute for it. Vendors demonstrating only single-agent surfaces will score 0 on Control Plane criteria.
3. The Scenario: End-to-End Talent Acquisition & Onboarding
A hiring manager at a WPP agency in USA needs to hire a Senior Data Engineer. The role is new; it requires budget approval, job design, sourcing, screening, interviewing, offer management, compliance checks, and onboarding. Today this process involves 8ÔÇô12 people across HR, Finance, IT, Legal and the hiring manager's own team; takes 45ÔÇô60 days; and is largely manual.
We want to see this compressed to days, not months: agents handling the operational work, humans making the requisite decisions from a Control Plane.
3.1 The Cast
Human participants (all interact via their preferred surface; all exceptions route to the Control Plane):
Hiring Manager (Los Angeles): initiates the request, makes final hiring decision
HR Business Partner (London): Control Plane operator; oversees policy compliance, approves exceptions across all active workflows
Finance Business Partner (Mumbai): validates budget, approves headcount spend
IT Operations (Chennai): provisions systems access and equipment
Candidate (external, location varies): applies, interviews, receives offer
Agent team (vendors must define composition; minimum suggested):
Orchestrator Agent: decomposes the request, builds task graph, manages workflow state, feeds Control Plane telemetry
Budget & Approvals Agent: checks headcount budget (Dataverse, Databricks), routes approval chain, tracks sign-off
Job Design Agent: drafts role profile, JD, levelling and comp benchmarking from internal sources (Dataverse, Databricks)
Sourcing Agent: searches internal talent pool, posts on external job boards, LinkedIn, referral networks (via APIs or single ATS integration i.e Greenhouse)
Triage Agent: parses CVs, scores against criteria, flags concerns, shortlists. Screening agent must excel at procedural memory handling (ÔÇ£for this role, we need PostgreSQL and Google Vertex experience within last 3 yearsÔÇØ)
Screening Agent: Deploys custom questionnaires to shortlist candidates (email, voice call (transcribed) confirming criteria
Interview Coordinator Agent: schedules across timezones, sends calendar invites, manages rescheduling
Compliance Agent: checks right-to-work, GDPR consent, EU AI Act obligations for automated hiring decisions
Offer Agent: generates offer letter from template, calculates comp package against band, routes for approval
Onboarding Agent: triggers JML workflow; provisions systems, schedules Day 1, assigns buddy, creates 30/60/90 plan
Additional agents may be proposed. Sub-agent spawning is encouraged (┬º1.3).
