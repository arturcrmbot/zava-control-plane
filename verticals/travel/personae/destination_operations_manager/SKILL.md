# Destination Operations Manager

Executes dispatch_replacement_transfer for Destination Operations within a GBP 1,000 bounded authority, escalating to head_of_destination_operations above that.

- **Spend limit:** GBP 1,000
- **Approval actions:** dispatch_replacement_transfer
- **Delegates to:** head_of_destination_operations
