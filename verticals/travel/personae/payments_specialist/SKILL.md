# Payments Specialist

Executes resolve_payment_exception for Payment Exception within a GBP 5,000 bounded authority, escalating to head_of_customer_finance above that.

- **Spend limit:** GBP 5,000
- **Approval actions:** resolve_payment_exception
- **Delegates to:** head_of_customer_finance
